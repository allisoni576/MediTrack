Implementation: Personal Medicine Management System
1. This App will track your medications, dosages, and schedules.
2. Users can input their medications, set reminders for when to take them, and log their intake.
3. The app will provide notifications to remind users to take their medications on time.
4. Users can view their medication history and track their adherence to their prescribed regimen.
5. The app will allow users to add reminders for refills.

Medication Details / Add Medication (4.1 & 5)
"Total quantity" — is this the total pill/unit count in the prescription (e.g., 90 tablets), used to calculate when a refill is needed, or just informational? Should the app auto-decrement it as doses are taken?
answer: Total quantity is the total pill/unit count in the prescription (e.g., 90 tablets) and should be used to calculate when a refill is needed. The app should auto-decrement it as doses are taken.

Dosage as integer — is this always "number of units per dose" (e.g., 2 tablets), regardless of medication form? Should there be a unit label (tablets, mL, pills) stored anywhere, or just a bare number?
answer: Dosage as an integer represents "number of units per dose" (e.g., 2 tablets), regardless of medication form. and should be used to calculate when a refill is needed. The app should auto-decrement it as doses are taken.

Refill info — "number of refills and refill dates" — is "number of refills" a fixed count (e.g., 3 refills allowed) with user manually entering specific dates for each, or is it meant to be auto-calculated from total quantity + dosage + frequency? Does the user manually add each refill date individually (list), or pick one next-refill date and a repeat count?
answer: "Number of refills" is a fixed count (e.g., 3 refills allowed) and the user should manually enter specific dates for each refill. The user can add each refill date individually in a list format.

Daily vs Weekly schedule — for "Daily," does that mean every single day while the medication is active (start–end date), with no day-of-week selection needed? Confirming Weekly requires at least one day checked.
answer: Yes, "Daily" means every single day while the medication is active (start–end date), with no day-of-week selection needed. For "Weekly," at least one day must be checked to indicate which days of the week the medication should be taken.

Times per day (up to 5) — must each of the up to 5 times be unique, and do they apply uniformly to every selected day (i.e., same times each day), or can different days have different times?
answer: Each of the up to 5 times must be unique, and they apply uniformly to every selected day (i.e., the same times each day). Different days cannot have different times.

Start/End dates — is end date optional (e.g., for ongoing/chronic medications with no end)?
answer: Yes, the end date is optional for ongoing/chronic medications with no specified end date.

Edit medication — when editing, can the user change frequency type / schedule, and should that regenerate/reschedule existing reminders and notifications?
answer: Yes, when editing, the user can change the frequency type/schedule. Changing the schedule should regenerate/reschedule existing reminders and notifications to align with the new schedule.



Reminders Screen (7)
"Custom reminder times and frequencies" — is this truly independent/custom per reminder, or does it just mean the reminder inherits the custom schedule configured per medication (from screen 5)? The instructions seem to say both ("fire based on the schedule that was set for the medication") — want to confirm reminders are purely derived from medication schedule, not separately editable.
answer: Reminders are purely derived from the medication schedule set in screen 5 and are not separately editable. The "custom reminder times and frequencies" refer to the schedule configured per medication, and reminders will fire based on that schedule.

Mark Taken checkbox vs button — instructions mention a checkbox to mark taken, while earlier sections say "mark taken or snooze" as actions/buttons. Should this be a checkbox (toggle) specifically, instead of a button?
answer: The "Mark Taken" action should be implemented as a button rather than a checkbox. This allows for a clear and distinct action for the user to indicate that they have taken their medication.

Snooze — is there a fixed snooze duration (e.g., 10/15/30 min), or should the user pick a duration each time?
answer: no, the snooze duration should be fixed (e.g., 15 minutes) to maintain consistency and simplicity in the user experience.

Notes on Reminders screen — are these general app-level/screen-level notes (one global notes field), or per-reminder notes tied to a specific dose/medication?
answer: Notes on the Reminders screen should be per-reminder notes tied to a specific dose/medication, allowing users to add relevant information or instructions for each individual reminder.

Adherence summary on Reminders screen — should this duplicate the Dashboard's adherence tracker, or show different scope (e.g., just today vs all-time)?
answer: The adherence summary on the Reminders screen should show a different scope, focusing on just today's adherence rather than duplicating the Dashboard's all-time adherence tracker. This allows users to quickly assess their performance for the current day.

Should RefillReminder notifications be separate from dose reminders in the notification channel/behavior, now that refill info is "number of refills + refill dates" rather than an interval?
answer: Yes, RefillReminder notifications should be separate from dose reminders in the notification channel/behavior. Since total quantity and dosage are now integers, should there be any validation (e.g., dosage can't exceed total quantity, warn when supply is low based on total quantity vs scheduled doses)?
answer: Yes, there should be validation to ensure that the dosage does not exceed the total quantity. This will help users manage their medications effectively and avoid running out of their prescriptions unexpectedly.
